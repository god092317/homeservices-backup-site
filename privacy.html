Privacy Policy

HomeServices Backup is a personal application used only by its owner.

The application uses Google Drive access only to create, read, update, and delete backup files created by the application in the owner's own Google Drive account.

Backup data is encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or made available to other users.

The application does not provide a public service and does not collect information from external users.

Google Drive access may be revoked by the owner at any time through their Google Account security settings.
